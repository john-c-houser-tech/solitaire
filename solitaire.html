How to play

Mouse

Drag
Move a card, or a run of cards, to another pile
Click, click
Pick a card up, then click where it goes
Double-click
Send a card to the best spot, foundations first
Stock
Click to deal; click the empty stock to turn the waste over
Finishing
Once every card is face up, the game plays the rest into the foundations by itself. Undo pauses this; a Finish button appears so you can restart it

Keyboard

← → ↑ ↓
Move the cursor. Up and down also choose how deep in a column
Space Enter
Pick up the card under the cursor, then drop it on the pile under the cursor. Press again on the same card to send it to the best spot
1–0
Drop the picked-up cards on that column, or jump the cursor there
F
Send the card to a foundation
C
Send the card to a free cell (FreeCell)
D
Deal from the stock
A
Move every playable card to the foundations
U Ctrl Z Backspace
Undo
Esc
Put the picked-up card back
N
New game
T
Next deck
? H
This panel

Choose a deck
